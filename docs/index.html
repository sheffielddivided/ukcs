    About field outlines
    Field outlines are NSTA's own authoritative field-determination boundaries (a separate dataset from the production figures) - a field with no matching boundary still shows as a marker. See methodology.

    How colour and size are derived
    Dominant commodity and marker size are by share of total_mboed, a derived analytical measure published in the underlying data (natural gas converted at 6,000 scf per barrel of oil equivalent - a conventional energy-equivalence factor, not a measured calorific value). Marker radius ∝ √(total_mboed). See methodology.
    Operator shown is the current operator of record, applied retrospectively to all periods (see spec section 6.1). Not necessarily who operated the field historically.
