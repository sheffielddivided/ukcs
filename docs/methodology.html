Methodology

← Back to the explorer

Production data

Field and operator production figures come from NSTA's "UKCS hydrocarbon field production reports PPRS points" dataset, rebuilt weekly. Storage volumes (e.g. Rough) and reporting-unit renames are explicitly separated from production totals, never silently merged. See data/meta.json for the exact source, resolved service URL, and build timestamp behind the figures currently on the site.

Equity-attributable production

Equity-attributable production is calculated by the weekly build (see data/equity/meta.json for the current build's figures) but is not yet shown anywhere in this site's charts or controls — that is a separate, not-yet-started step. This page documents the methodology behind the artifacts that already exist, ahead of that integration.

Source

Equity-attributable production is calculated from the NSTA dataset whose ArcGIS item is titled “Field Partners”. The NSTA Fields data theme page describes this same dataset as “Current and historical field equity shares”. Both names are recorded as provenance in every build (source_item_title and source_page_description in data/equity/meta.json): the item title is a publisher naming choice, not a description of the data's temporal scope, and the workbook's actual content — dated ownership intervals back to the late 1960s — has been verified directly rather than assumed from either name. Contains information provided by the North Sea Transition Authority and/or other third parties — see the attribution notice in this site's footer and ATTRIBUTION.md.

Interval resolution

Ownership is resolved once per calendar month, at the first day of the month (“month-start”), using half-open intervals: an interest is active for a given month if start_date <= month_start < end_date. An open (blank) end date means the interest is still current. Ownership changing mid-month is attributed entirely to whichever interest was in force on day 1 of that month — never prorated.

Zero-duration and zero-interest rows

A non-trivial share of source rows record an identical start and end date. These are retained as source records but are never treated as an active ownership interval for any month — a zero-duration interval cannot satisfy the half-open test above for any month-start. Separately, some rows record a company's involvement in a field with a recorded interest of 0%. These are also retained as source records but contribute no equity-attributable production, since production multiplied by a 0% share is 0 regardless of the row's other attributes.

Operator status

The source separately records which company operates a field (operator_flag in the field artifacts). Operator status does not imply a positive economic interest — a substantial share of 0%-interest rows belong to the recorded operator. Operator status is never used as a proxy for ownership.

Unresolved field-months

Where a field's ownership records for a given month cannot be resolved cleanly — most commonly because two records genuinely overlap and together claim more than 100% of the field — that field-month is excluded from the calculation entirely, not repaired, normalised back to 100%, or resolved by preferring one recorded owner over another. This is reported in data/equity/anomalies.json, not silently absorbed into any total.

Coverage

Coverage is measured and shown separately for each production stream (oil, dry gas, associated gas, condensate) as the share of that stream's total reported field production for which ownership was cleanly resolved that month. Streams are never combined into a single boe/d figure.

Publication window

Equity ownership records are not evenly available across the production history this site otherwise covers back to 1975. Coverage in earlier years is materially incomplete — the underlying NSTA equity dataset was not populated retroactively to most fields' actual production start dates. Equity-attributable production is therefore published only from March 2013 onward — the earliest month for which coverage was verified to be consistently high across a stable multi-year run, not merely high in one good year.

A stream-month below the 95% threshold is never shown as zero — it is represented explicitly as unavailable, with its coverage percentage and exclusion reasons preserved, so a chart shows a gap rather than a misleading flat line. These thresholds apply separately by production stream, never as a single combined figure. The publication start date does not move automatically in later builds; changing it requires an explicit methodology decision and an accompanying test update (see etl/equity_config.py).

MURLACH [pt of MARNOCK-SKUA] — known limitation

This field's only recorded equity rows have an unexplained start date of 2050-01-04. No authoritative evidence links its ownership to the related but distinct MARNOCK [pt. of MARNOCK-SKUA] entry in the source, so none is assumed. MURLACH is excluded from equity-attributable totals until this is resolved by better source evidence; its excluded production is included in stream coverage calculations and fully documented in data/equity/anomalies.json.

No parent-company rollup

Every company name in the equity artifacts is the legal entity exactly as recorded by NSTA in the source workbook. There is no mapping to a current corporate parent or group, and similarly-named entities (e.g. two subsidiaries of the same group) are never merged.

What this figure is not

Equity-attributable production is a gross equity share of reported field production — the field's reported volume multiplied by each company's recorded ownership percentage for that month. It is not net production, working-interest production, or entitlement production: it carries no adjustment for royalty, tax, or any other entitlement mechanism, and should not be read as a substitute for those figures.

Known limitation, both datasets

Every check in this project's build is an internal-consistency check against the data it received from NSTA — paginated correctly, counted correctly, internally coherent. None of them can independently verify that what NSTA served was itself complete. A service returning a dataset that is internally consistent but silently incomplete is not detectable by this pipeline. This is an open, unmitigated gap for both the production and equity sources, not a solved one.
